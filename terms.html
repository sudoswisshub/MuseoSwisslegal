1. Overview & Service Description

MuseoSwiss is an editorial guide and educational mobile application for exploring Swiss art, historical photography, artist archives, and cultural heritage monuments. Features include digital collection browsing, offline favorites, location-assisted nearby discovery, on-device audio narration, trivia, and daily widgets.

2. Intellectual Property & Open Data Licences

MuseoSwiss presents authoritative cultural records obtained from official public institutions under open data frameworks:

  • Swiss National Library (Gugelmann & Annemarie Schwarzenbach Collections): Open Government Data (OGD) licensed under public domain or creative commons frameworks as declared by the provider.
  • SIK-ISEA SIKART Export: Artist biography records and lexicon metadata provided by SIK-ISEA under official export permissions.
  • Swisstopo GeoAdmin REST API: Federal Inventory of Heritage Sites of National Importance (ISOS) and Inventory of Cultural Property under Swisstopo open data terms.

Original source rights, institutional credits, and individual copyright terms attached to specific archival works are preserved and displayed within each item’s details panel. All app code, custom editorial layouts, design elements, branding, and app interfaces remain the exclusive property of MuseoSwiss.
